Hourly ECMWF IFS (9 km) precipitation, snowfall, snow depth, shortwave and temperature on a 0.1° grid, interpolated bilinearly in space and linearly in time. Terrain is lit by the computed sun position for each hour, using true (unexaggerated) slope normals, so sun-facing slopes brighten. The shortwave mode spreads gridded horizontal radiation onto slopes (20% diffuse, 80% direct beam × incidence, an approximation). Snow thickness is exaggerated 150×. Per-slope snow depth is illustrative: the cell depth is scaled by exposure to a midday sun, so sunnier slopes clear first. Trickles follow steepest descent on the DEM (conceptual runoff paths).
