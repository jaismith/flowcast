flowcast

River forecasts, 1 hour to 7 days

How are the rivers running?

Every dot is a USGS gauge in flowcast's model, colored by how today's flow compares with normal for the date. Pick a featured river to see its forecasts.

Featured rivers

Three rivers that behave very differently: a big regulated river, a snowmelt river and a flashy creek.

What you're looking at

Current conditions come straight from USGS gauges. flowcast's forecasts come from one model trained on 550+ rivers across the eastern US: a neural network that reads rain, snow, sunlight and the river's own recent flow, then issues a probabilistic forecast out to 7 days.

Why the forecasts are replays

Live forecasts aren't published yet. Each river page replays how the model would have forecast real events in October 2020 – September 2022, years it never saw in training. That's also how the skill numbers are measured.
