Rain, snowfall, snow depth and shortwave radiation are hourly ECMWF IFS (9 km) on a 0.1° grid, interpolated with a smooth cubic B-spline between cells (which softens peaks) and linearly between hours. Each bright tracer is one parcel of new rain or snowmelt (melt = hourly drop in snow depth × 0.3 density). It lands, trickles to the nearest stream at a conceptual hillslope speed of 0.1 m/s, then travels downstream at NHDPlus velocity scaled by flow. Sunlit-slope glow marks slopes that face the sun (true solar position) more squarely than flat ground, scaled by gridded shortwave. Faint dots are gauge-derived base flow, as in v1.
