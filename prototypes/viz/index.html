flowcast · visualization prototypes

Seven interactive sketches of how flowcast could show the water moving through the upper Delaware: where rain and snowmelt land, which streams they feed, and how confident the forecast is at the Callicoon gauge (USGS 01427510). Each prototype tries a different approach, from D3 particle maps to three.js terrain to scrollytelling.

They all run on real public data cached at build time. Nothing on these pages calls a paid API.

Data: USGS NWIS (discharge, water temperature) · USGS NLDI + NHDPlus V2 (basin, flowlines, reservoirs, flow paths) · Open-Meteo (ERA5 reanalysis, GEFS ensemble, GloFAS flood ensemble) · AWS Terrain Tiles · Esri World Imagery (scrollytelling only) · flowcast's own /forecast endpoint (read-only, cached). Refresh with npm run fetch-data.
